UniMate
Find Your People. Find Your Place.
UniMate is a personalized campus experience that helps students discover the right people, study groups, communities, and activities without having to search across different platforms.

Features

People Discovery: Connect with like-minded students.
Study Groups: Find peers for academic collaboration.
Communities: Explore groups based on shared interests.
Campus Activities: Discover events and university opportunities.
Personalization: Find connections and activities that match your interests.

Technologies:
HTML5
CSS3
avaScript
