Why UniMate exists

One campus.
Too many places to look.

University life should feel connected, but finding the right people, communities and experiences can often mean searching across multiple platforms.

Problem

Campus life is fragmented.

University students face a fragmented digital experience, having to use multiple platforms to find study partners, communities, people with shared interests, and campus activities. This can make it difficult especially for new students to find compatible connections, get involved, and know where to begin.

Solution

UniMate brings it together.

UniMate brings people, study groups, communities, and campus activities into one personalized experience, helping students discover connections, join communities, and stay informed about upcoming events.

Research

What the research tells us
44%

Feel lonely at university

Large class sizes, remote learning, and packed schedules leave little room for meaningful connection.

1 in 4

Fall short of their expectations

Students participate in clubs and activities significantly less than they intended when starting university.

25%

Never get involved

One in four students never engage with campus societies, sports teams, or peer support groups.

#1

Friction is the real barrier

The problem isn't a lack of events or clubs it's fragmented systems that make them hard to discover.

Why it matters

University life is meant to be transformative, yet structural hurdles leave thousands of students feeling disconnected from campus culture.

Isolation harms well-being

When students struggle to navigate large campuses or balance demanding schedules, isolation sets in, and that can weigh on mental well-being, motivation, and academic performance.

Unlocking Existing Value

Universities spend vast resources on societies and support groups. Lowering the discovery friction allows students to tap into opportunities that already exist.

Fulfilling the University Experience

Higher education isn't just about degrees, it's about community. Creating a true sense of belonging transforms university from a chore into an inspiring journey.
